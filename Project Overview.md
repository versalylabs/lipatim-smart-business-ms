\# **Project Overview**: LipaTim

\*\*Tagline:\*\* Smart Business Management. Seamless eTIMS Compliance.

\*\*Product Type:\*\* Lightweight ERP, Inventory \& Tax Compliance Suite

\*\*Location Focus:\*\* Nairobi, Kenya (Optimized for local tax laws and payment behaviors)



\---



\## 1. Executive Summary

\*\*LipaTim\*\* is an all-in-one business management tool designed specifically for Kenyan SMEs. It simplifies the complexity of daily operations—sales, inventory, and payments—while ensuring 100% automated compliance with KRA’s eTIMS requirements. By integrating M-Pesa and eTIMS directly into the point of sale, LipaTim removes the manual work of double-entry and tax filing.



\---



\## 2. Core Modules



\### A. Smart Inventory Management

\*   \*\*Real-Time Tracking:\*\* Monitor stock levels across one or multiple branches.

\*   \*\*Stock-In/Stock-Out:\*\* Detailed logging of purchases from suppliers and sales to customers.

\*   \*\*Expiry Tracking:\*\* Critical for chemists and grocery retail to minimize losses.

\*   \*\*Low Stock Triggers:\*\* Automated SMS alerts when items reach a predefined reorder level.



\### B. eTIMS-Compliant POS \& Invoicing

\*   \*\*One-Click eTIMS Invoices:\*\* Every sale automatically generates a KRA-validated invoice with the required QR code and signature.

\*   \*\*Digital Receipts:\*\* Send receipts directly to customers via WhatsApp or Email, or print to Bluetooth thermal printers.

\*   \*\*Multi-Device Access:\*\* Cashiers can sell using Android tablets, smartphones, or desktop computers.

\*   \*\*Credit Management:\*\* Track customer debts (denim) and send automated payment reminders via SMS.



\### C. M-Pesa \& Payment Integration

\*   \*\*M-Pesa STK Push:\*\* Initiate payment requests directly from the POS to the customer’s phone.

\*   \*\*Till/Paybill Reconciliation:\*\* Automated matching of M-Pesa transactions to specific sales, eliminating manual book-checking at the end of the day.

\*   \*\*Split Payments:\*\* Support for transactions paid via a mix of Cash and M-Pesa.



\### D. Expense \& Supplier Management

\*   \*\*Opex Tracking:\*\* Log business costs such as rent, electricity, and staff wages.

\*   \*\*Supplier Ledger:\*\* Manage accounts payable and track purchase history to negotiate better prices with wholesalers.



\### E. Analytics \& Reporting

\*   \*\*Z-Reports:\*\* Generate daily end-of-shift reports showing cash vs. M-Pesa vs. Credit.

\*   \*\*VAT Ready Reports:\*\* Export monthly summaries that align perfectly with KRA iTax requirements, making filing a 5-minute task.

\*   \*\*Profit \& Loss:\*\* Real-time visibility into the actual health of the business.



\---



\## 3. Technical Specifications



\*   \*\*Offline-First Architecture:\*\* LipaTim allows businesses to continue selling even during Safaricom or ISP outages. Data syncs to the cloud and KRA servers once the connection is restored.

\*   \*\*Direct API Integration:\*\* 

&#x20;   \*   \*\*KRA eTIMS API:\*\* Built on the VSCU/OSCU framework.

&#x20;   \*   \*\*Safaricom Daraja API:\*\* For automated M-Pesa processing.

\*   \*\*Security:\*\* Multi-level user permissions (Cashier, Manager, Admin) to prevent internal fraud and stock shrinkage.

\*   \*\*Cloud Hosting:\*\* Secure, encrypted data backup to ensure business records are never lost.
